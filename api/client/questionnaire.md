## Products and Pricing

- What products do you sell and what categories should customers browse?
- Do products have options such as age, clothing size, color, material, or another variant?
- What should the option be called in the store (for example, “Age” or “Size”)?
- Does each option have its own price? If so, provide a sample product and its option/price list.
- Do products have stock quantities, one-of-a-kind availability, or made-to-order availability?
- Which product details should be shown (description, materials, care, fit, condition, measurements)?
- What currency and price format should the store use?
- Do prices include tax? If not, what tax rules should apply?
- Please provide product photos, descriptions, categories, and starting prices.

## Checkout and Payments

- What exact message should customers see after submitting an order?
- What percentage is due as an advance payment?
- Which payment methods do you accept (bank transfer, cash on delivery, card, wallet, other)?
- For bank transfer, provide the public payment instructions and account display details.
- Should customers upload payment proof? Which file types and maximum size are acceptable?
- When is the remaining balance due?
- What order terms, cancellation/refund policy, or payment disclaimer must customers accept?
- What customer details are required at checkout (name, email, phone, address, delivery notes)?

## Fulfillment and Communications

- Which areas do you deliver to, and what are the delivery charges?
- What are the usual preparation and delivery timelines?
- How should customers receive order, payment, and shipping updates?
- What sender name, reply-to address, and store email should appear in notifications?
- Who should receive new order and payment notifications?
- What should happen when an item is sold out or unavailable?

## Store Administration

- Who will manage products, orders, and payment verification?
- What administrator name and email should be associated with the store?
- Are there existing products, customer accounts, or order records to migrate?
- Is there any required legal, privacy, accessibility, or age-related wording?
- Any examples of stores or design references that capture the intended look?
